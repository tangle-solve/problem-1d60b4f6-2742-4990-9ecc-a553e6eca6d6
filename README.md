### ALGO-000175: Sakoda–Sipser two-way automata problem
- **Subfield:** Games, automata, and verification
- **Open:** Determine whether every \(n\)-state two-way nondeterministic finite automaton has an equivalent two-way deterministic automaton with polynomially many states.
- **Status:** Open since 1978. Restricted alphabets and automaton classes have partial results; the general state-complexity question remains unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Isolates the power of nondeterminism in a very small computational model.
- **Key works:** Sakoda and Sipser (1978), *Nondeterminism and the Size of Two Way Finite Automata*, DOI: 10.1145/800133.804357; Pighizzini (2013), DOI: 10.3233/FI-2013-879.
